# V18 Accessibility & Publication Evidence Setup

Apply after V13–V17 in a dedicated VR Classroom Studio Supabase project.

V18 adds optional evidence tables:
- `xr_accessibility_reports`
- `xr_publication_reports`

These tables preserve authoring-side QA evidence only. They do not certify WCAG conformance and they do not replace institutional accessibility review.

Blackboard playback remains standalone through the exported SCORM package.
